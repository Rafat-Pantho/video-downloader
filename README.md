## Features

- Modern Dark UI - Sleek dark theme with glassmorphism design
- Collapsible Sidebar - Toggle navigation to maximize workspace
- Built-in Login - Download private/age-restricted videos without browser extensions
- Cookie Management - View, edit, and manage cookies for authenticated downloads
- Multi-Platform Support - YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, and 1000+ sites
- Quality Options - Best, 1080p, 720p, 480p, or Audio Only
- Multiple Formats - MP4, MKV, WEBM, AVI (video) | MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV (audio)
- Real-time Progress - Live download progress with percentage
- Powered by yt-dlp - The most reliable video downloader

## Quick Start

1. **Download** the installer for your platform
2. **Install** the application
3. **Paste** a video URL
4. **Click** "Fetch Info" to preview the video
5. **Select** quality and format
6. **Download!**

## Private/Restricted Videos

The app supports downloading private and age-restricted videos:

1. Click **Cookie Management** in the sidebar
2. Click **Login** and sign in to the website
3. Close the login window
4. Return to Home and download your video
